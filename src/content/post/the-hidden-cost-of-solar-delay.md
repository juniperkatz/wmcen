John Pepi turned the group's forest-and-solar work into a nine-minute narrated talk.

The argument is blunt. If Massachusetts protects forest land but builds solar too slowly to meet its climate plan, it pays twice. Less clean electricity today, and a larger forest carbon burden tomorrow. A 20 percent solar shortfall over 2026 to 2050 leaves the state needing roughly 88,000 additional forest acres just to absorb the carbon it failed to avoid.
