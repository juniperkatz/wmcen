We are a small, informal group of Western Massachusetts residents working on clean energy, siting, and forest stewardship. Members include academics, consultants, business owners, nonprofit volunteers, and retired community members with decades of local knowledge. We meet in person and on email.

The Massachusetts energy transition is happening unevenly across the state. Cities have rooftops. Rural towns have land. The state law that took effect in 2024 sets new rules about who decides what. We exist to help residents and town officials understand those rules and the choices they create.

We do not represent any agency, university, or company. Members are speaking for themselves.
